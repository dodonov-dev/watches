"Oval wedge". Real size: 56 mm wide, 108 mm tall, 50 mm deep at the desk and about 26 mm deep at the top. Every horizontal section is the same soft oval family as the oval shell (a superellipse: flattened sides, generously rounded, no corners), only the depth shrinks with height. The back is vertical and gently curved like the oval's back. The front leans back 15 degrees from vertical and is gently curved across its width (like the oval's front, not a flat plane); seen from the side the front is a straight line leaning back. The top is flat, oval in outline, with a soft 6 mm rounded edge all round. The two tubes sit in the leaning front, centred, starting about 22 mm above the desk. A tiny 14 mm "EH" logo is centred on the front below the tubes, as a soft glow through the thin wall.
