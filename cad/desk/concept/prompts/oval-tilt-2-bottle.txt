"Leaning bottle". Take the oval shell exactly (56 mm wide, 36 mm deep, 110 mm along its axis, oval plan like a bar of soap, flat top with a soft 6 mm rounded edge, curved front and back) and lean the WHOLE body back by 15 degrees, like a bottle leaning against nothing. Front, sides and back all lean together and stay parallel. The bottom is cut flat parallel to the desk so it stands; the top stays perpendicular to the body's axis (so it faces up and slightly back). It should look poised and deliberate, not falling: a calm, confident lean. The two tubes sit in the leaning front, centred. A tiny 14 mm "EH" logo is centred on the front below the tubes, as a soft glow through the thin wall.
