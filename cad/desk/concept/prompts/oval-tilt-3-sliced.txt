"Sliced oval". An upright oval column (56 mm wide, 48 mm deep, 108 mm tall; the oval shell's soft soap-bar plan, vertical curved sides and back, a flat top with a soft 6 mm rounded edge) whose front has been sliced off by ONE plane leaning back 15 degrees. The slice leaves a flat front panel with an oval-ish outline (wider at the bottom, narrower at the top, following where the plane cuts the curved body); its border is softly rounded (about 3 mm) where it meets the curved sides, so it reads like a polished facet on a pebble. Near the desk the column is full depth; at the top the slice has taken most of the front, so the top is a shorter oval. The two tubes sit in the flat sliced panel, centred. A tiny 14 mm "EH" logo glows through the wall on the panel below the tubes.
