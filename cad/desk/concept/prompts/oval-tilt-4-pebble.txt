"Leaning pebble". A fully soft, monolithic form like a smooth river stone standing up and leaning back: 60 mm wide, 55 mm deep at the desk, 104 mm tall. The front is a long, gently convex face leaning back about 15 degrees (nearly flat where the tubes are, curving away only at its edges). The back is not vertical: it rounds over in one continuous curve from the top down to the desk, like the back of a seal or a thumb. There is no flat top and no edge anywhere: every section is a soft oval, every transition a large radius. It sits on a small flat foot that is hidden in its own shadow. The two tubes sit in the front, centred. A tiny 14 mm "EH" logo glows through the wall low on the front below the tubes.
