ROLY-POLY: BUILD SHEET, round 8: face tilted back 8 deg, pebble-like front with lipped slots, a new rocking cup
(files in cad/desk/out-rolypoly/; numbers in report.txt). The round 6 / 7 cups do NOT fit this body (its front at the seam is
new): print the new cup-rock. The flat cup is dropped.

PRINT  (Bambu X1E, Bambu Studio, 0.4 nozzle)
  body.stl        PLA Matte white, 0.08 mm (0.08 HQ), 6 walls. Seam ring on the plate, crown up. Supports: tree, on build
                  plate only (as round 7: they improved the slots' top ends): for the slots' top ends (54 mm2; each a
                  bridge 8.1 wide at the inner land, 13.5 at the face, its end face 8 deg off straight down),
                  the crown's inside (452 mm2) and the cable notch's arch. The outside has no overhang steeper than
                  45 deg (0.0 mm2; it leans out at most 31 deg). Block supports in the logo pocket
                  (its top flank leans 40 deg from vertical). The inner land's lower edge rises 50 deg.
                  No brim on the seam ring. enable_overhang_speed 0 as round 7's plate (closure-ring test: B best).
  cup-rock.stl    PLA Matte white, 0.08. On its back as round 7, tipped 35 deg toward its rim (to-print/mkplate8.py,
                  CUP_TILT): it stands on the rim's back edge; round 7's ring patch moves from the front to the
                  lower front by the keel; tree supports (mostly inside the bowl) + 8 mm brim on the back edge.
                  (Lying on its front would put the support cradle on the front.) Sand the rolling band lightly if a seam shows.
  arc-gauge.stl   any PLA, flat side down (optional).
  cast-plug.stl   any PLA, handle up (it stands on its sloped bottom: a few support pins, or print it handle down).
  logo-stencil.stl  3 stencils (webs 0.72, 1.00, 1.40), as round 7b: flat back on the plate,
                  no supports; paint the one you use black.

FINISH
  Slots: a rod (8.0) should drop in with a little play (8.1 slot, 0.6 land at the inner face, then the round lips); if
  tight, a light pass with a flat needle file on the land only, not on the lips. Check the inner land behind the glass is
  clean (no blobs at the slots' ends). Break the seam ring's outer edge lightly with 400 so body and cup meet without a lip.

PARTS
  As round 7: 12 magnets D3.0 x 2.0 and 2 dowels (new holes: the cup is new), the right-angle USB-C extension,
  the logo stencil, two warm-white 0603 LEDs + an SH1.0 4-pin pigtail, Kapton, double-sided foam tape (1 mm) for the battery,
  gasket tape (0.5) for the glass's border, a soft foam pad, OCA for the rods, 2 rods 8 x 4 half-round cut to 66.65,
  the 304 balls and 5-minute epoxy, stick-on weights.

ASSEMBLY (in this order; everything goes in from below with the cup off; "along the lean" = parallel to the face, 8 deg
back from vertical)
  1. Body: magnets and dowels in the seam bosses (flush in glue); the cup's magnets likewise.
  2. Logo, then LEDs: as round 7b (stencil in from inside along the pocket's axis, a drop of glue on its back edge; LEDs in
     the room 4.5-8.5 mm behind the logo).
  3. Board: rods on the glass (OCA, centred on the tubes), gasket tape on the glass's border. Plug the LED pigtail into the I2C
     header and switch the board ON first. Bring the board in through the seam opening, glass to the front, and push it up
     ALONG THE LEAN keeping it 4.2 mm behind its seat (the rods' crowns 0.5 behind the inner land; checked >=
     0.3 on the way), up to its height, then forward: the rods drop into their slots (0.05 a side), the glass
     presses onto the land.
  4. Battery, from below after the board, taped to its back as round 7: Kapton on the board's back, foam tape on the battery
     (liner on), its lead plugged first; push it up along the lean 0.5 mm behind its seat, centred at x -0.5 (the
     centre of the seam opening), until it is 1.2 mm in, then 0.5 mm to the
     left and on up to its height (its bottom 1 mm over where the USB head will be); peel
     the liner and press it onto the board's back. Checked: >= 0.36 to the body
     on the way; its top corners are the tight spot.
  5. USB: push the extension's right-angle plug up into the board's port from below, along the lean (checked:
     2.50 to the body, 1.00 to the battery), cable towards the back, bent 8 deg at the head
     to run level into the round notch in the back rim (x -5).
  6. Hold-down: as round 7 (gasket tape, rods in their slots, a foam pad between the battery's back and the back wall left of
     x +5).
  7. Cast (cup off), as round 7: three batches of 5-minute epoxy to 12.5 / 8.5 / 5.0 mm
     below the seam face; 108 g of balls in all (100 g is still fine). NEW: before the last batch sets, press
     the cast plug (cast-plug.stl, wrapped in packing tape or greased) into it under where the USB head's far end will be: its
     flat top flush with the cast's top, its long side along y at x -5 (dummy-usb-head.stl shows the place); pull it out
     after the cure. It leaves the dimple the tilted head needs (0.5 clear). 24 h cure.
  8. Trim layer: 3 segments fit on the cast (dummy-segments-rock.stl). The plan (nominal masses): stick
     3 segment(s) standing on the back inner wall at x +28, +13, +18 (bottoms at z
     25.5, 28.5, 48.0, i.e. 3.5, 6.5, 26.0 mm above the seam
     plane; fill the gap behind each one's middle, up to 2.0 mm, with foam tape), leave out
     0 of the trim layer and slide the rest
     73 % of their rows' slack to the right. Do not peel the adhesive yet.
  9. Cup on, straight up: the cable slides into the cup's notch, pins find the holes, magnets close the seam. Trim on the
     table, then stick the segments down.
  To service: cup off, unplug the USB head; battery and board come out the way they went in (along the lean).

TRIM
  Level table, everything wired and closed. The face leans 8 deg back now: put the phone level on the back of the head or
  on the table edge, not on the face. Coarse: a segment at the back-wall spots. Fine: slide a row of segments towards the high
  side: 10 mm of one 5 g segment moves the lean by about 1.0 deg.

CHECK ON THE PRINT
  the front (flat over the slots, no brow, no shelf, the seam's front), the lips and the slots' top ends, the rods' crowns
  standing proud (3 front, 0.55..0.75), the board's and battery's ways in along the lean, the USB cable's bend at
  the head, rocking and falling forward (beyond 33 deg) / backward (beyond 26 deg, calculated), the
  stencil's fit and the logo glow.
