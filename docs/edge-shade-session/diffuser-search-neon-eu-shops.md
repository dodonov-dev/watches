### Top 3 Picks for a Hobbyist Test (8 mm × 70 mm Acrylic Half-Rod on LCD Glass)

1. **`elbfolien.de` LED-Diffusorfolie selbstklebend (20% & 30% diffusion variants, 80 µm)** + **clear 50 µm transfer tape (`SPRINTIS OL05` from €2.98 or `3M 924` from €11.75 at `sprintis.de`, or `3M 467MP` at `digikey.de`)**
   - **Why:** Best optical control for *slightly* softening an LCD pixel grid without crushing brightness or adding paper-fiber grain. You can request **free samples** (or buy tiny custom cuts starting around €1.08) to lay 20%, 30%, and 60% side-by-side on your live LCD before bonding. Because it is single-sided adhesive, bond the diffuser film to the LCD glass and laminate the acrylic half-rod on top with a 50 µm clear acrylic transfer tape.

2. **`tesa 4959` double-sided translucent non-woven (Vlies) tape (12 mm × 50 m, 100 µm) from RS Components (`de.rs-online.com`, RS `207-411`, €10.55 ex VAT / €12.55 incl. VAT, MOQ 1)** *(budget alternative: `Lohmann DuploCOLL 200` at `klebeshop24.de` from €5.93, 150 µm)*
   - **Why:** Simplest **one-step** test — no film-plus-tape sandwich required. At 12 mm wide it trims easily to 8 mm × 70 mm, is only 100 µm thick, uses aging-/yellowing-resistant modified acrylic adhesive suitable for glass and PMMA, and ships in MOQ 1 from a major EU distributor. *Caveat:* the non-woven paper carrier has microscopic fibers, which may look slightly textured over a very high-PPI LCD viewed up close.

3. **`3M Scotchcal 7725-314` (Dusted Crystal, fine frosted, ~50–81 µm, 85% VLT per 3M TDS) from `foliencenter24.com` (~€41.20 / 1 m × 1.22 m)** — *or lower-cost test alternatives: `selbstklebefolien.com` Diffusionsfolie 46% (free sample / €24.99/m) or `plotterfolie.de` ORACAL 8510 (from €2.34)*
   - **Why:** Among true smooth, fiber-free matte/frosted films, **3M 7725-314** has the highest documented light transmission (**85% VLT**), avoiding the heavy dimming of 25–30% LED signbox diffusers. Pair with clear 50 µm transfer tape to attach the half-rod. *Important optical caveat for any surface-frosted film (including 7725-314, ORACAL 8510, and Scotch 810):* if you laminate acrylic adhesive directly onto the textured matte face, the index-matched adhesive can partially "wet out" the micro-roughness and reduce haze — test a small laminated coupon first to verify it retains enough diffusion (internal/bulk-scattering films like the `elbfolien.de` / `selbstklebefolien.com` diffusers are far less affected by adhesive wet-out).
