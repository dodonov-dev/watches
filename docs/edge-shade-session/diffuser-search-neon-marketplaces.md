### Key Market Reality (Before the Table)

* **True double-sided hazy / light-diffusing OCA** (scattering particles suspended *inside* an optically clear PSA matrix) exists industrially (see manufacturer datasheets below), **but is not stocked in small hobbyist quantities** on Amazon, AliExpress, eBay, DigiKey, Mouser, RS, or low-MOQ Alibaba listings. Standard off-the-shelf double-sided OCAs (3M 8146/8171/8211, Nitto LUCIACS, tesa 880xx, Lohmann DuploCOLL OC) are all engineered for ultra-low haze (`<1%`).
* **Orderable hobbyist alternatives** fall into two groups:
  1. **One adhesive face + one matte/frosted face** (anti-glare PET display films and translucent etched-glass films). *Optical caveat:* if the diffusion comes from **surface roughness** rather than internal particles, wet-bonding clear OCA/UV glue onto the matte face to attach the acrylic half-rod ($n \approx 1.49$) will index-match the roughness and erase most of the haze—keep a dry contact/micro air gap under the rod (bond at the ends) unless the film is internally pigmented.
  2. **DIY stack:** clear double-sided OCA sheets + a separate diffuser film (same index-matching caveat if the diffuser is surface-matte rather than bulk-scattering).

### Top 3 Picks for a Hobbyist Test

1. **Photodon Build-Your-Own Sample Pack (`Sample-Films-BYO`) — order `MXG` (<35% haze, >90% TLT, 140 µm), `HSG` (35% haze, 90% TLT, 280 µm), and `MXT` (15±2% haze, ≥92% TLT, 100 µm) in `3" × 5"`**
   * **Why:** It is the only hobbyist-orderable product found with **published, measured haze % right around 15–35% AND >90% total light transmittance**, specifically formulated for LCD displays (low sparkle). For `$12` shipped to the EU (`$7 + $5`), one `3" × 5"` (~76 × 127 mm) sample easily covers multiple `8 × 70 mm` test strips and lets you A/B test three haze strengths. *Mount note:* stick the adhesive side to the LCD glass and keep the matte side dry against the acrylic half-rod (edge-tack/clamp the rod ends) so adhesive doesn't index-match out the surface texture.
2. **3M™ Scotchcal™ Glass Design Film `7725-314` (Dusted Crystal) from Foliencenter24** *(or ask Foliencenter24 for a `Muster`/hand sample of both `7725-314` and `3M Fasara SH2MACRX2`)*
   * **Why:** Ships within the EU with zero customs friction, thin (`~81 µm` total incl. clear acrylic PSA), and **high visible light transmittance (`85%` on clear glass; `86%` for `SH2MACRX2`)**—much brighter than sign-making LED diffusers (30–66%). Note that `7725-314` is cast PVC and `SH2MACRX2` is PET; requesting hand samples from Foliencenter24 first avoids paying `€41–€117` for a full meter when you only need `8 × 70 mm`.
3. **ASLAN® Diffuser Film `D 160` (Foliencenter24 from `€24.00` or Modulor from `€31.90` for 0.5 m)** — *only if you must wet-bond the acrylic rod on both sides*
   * **Why:** Unlike surface-etched matte films, `D 160` (`85 µm` polymeric PVC, acrylic PSA) uses **internal pigment/bulk diffusion**, so bonding clear OCA or UV resin between its top face and the acrylic half-rod will not erase the scattering effect. The trade-off is heavy brightness loss (`~46%` light transmission), so treat it as a mechanical/optical wet-bonding proof-of-concept—and if bulk-diffusing OCA proves necessary without losing brightness, email LINTEC Opteria (`MO-8017`) or Tomoegawa (`DA Series`) for an engineering sample sheet.
