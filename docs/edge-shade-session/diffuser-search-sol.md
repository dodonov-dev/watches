For your bonded half-rod, one limitation matters: **adding clear adhesive over a matte scattering surface can substantially reduce its diffusion by filling the surface texture**. That mechanism is documented in [3M’s optical-film patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11294106B2/en). Whether it happens with each retail film above remains untested; their published haze values are not measurements of your completed sandwich.

**Top three hobbyist tests**

1. **Photodon HSG in a 3 × 5″ sample pack**, preferably alongside MXH. HSG has the strongest documented optical fit: **35% haze and 90% transmission**. Its **280 ± 10 µm thickness exceeds your limit**, and it has only one adhesive face.
2. **Velken 551030, 9 × 9 cm sample.** The easiest EU purchase, with published transmission, thickness and haze. Its **11.9% haze** provides a useful mild-softening baseline.
3. **BitTradeOne MMDFSF.** A **€3.95**, **80 µm** purpose-made adhesive diffuser with Eurozone shipping. Worth a low-cost experiment, but haze, transmission and suitability for bonding beneath the rod remain unverified.
