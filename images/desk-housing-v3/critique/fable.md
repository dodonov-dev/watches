# Relief critique (Fable)

## 1. Ranking

1. **(2) WIP** – mixed facet sizes, intact silhouette, reads as worked by hand; its flaws are long vertical strips on the sides (the "whittled wood" cue) and facets leaking onto the web between the rods.
2. **(1) Smooth A** – honest and beautiful, but white + flawless + blue scale is exactly the thermometer look.
3. **(5) WIP reproduced** – right family, wrong scale: 2.6 mm planes on a ~30 mm radius are ~0.03 mm deep, under one layer; sanding returns it to A, and where it survives it looks like a low-res STL.
4. **(4) River** – no ridges for matte white to catch, so it reads as sink marks or a warped print, not water-worn stone.
5. **(3) 8 mm / 1 mm facets** – breaks the silhouette, crystal top, game-asset rock; hard seam against the smooth front.

## 2. Pick: (2), redrawn deliberately

- **Facets:** 6–12 mm, mixed sizes, 4–6-sided and roughly equiaxed. No long triangles, no shared vertical direction: that removes the wood.
- **Depth:** 5–9° between neighbours, 0.3–0.5 mm over the base surface, silhouette deviation at most 0.5 mm. Model ridges crisp; sanding will round them. Anything under 0.2 mm will not survive.
- **Dome and back shoulders:** full strength, larger facets (10–14 mm). This is where the WIP photo is best.
- **Sides:** medium.
- **Front panel, brow, chin, logo:** zero, feathered out over 8–10 mm before the panel edge, never a cut line.
- **Base:** bottom 4 mm clean, for a calm foot line.
- **Sanding:** hard block, one facet at a time. A sponge melts it all back to A.

## 3. Idea not in the sheet: honed, pitted skin

Outward-only fuzzy skin (~0.2 mm) painted on sides, back and dome, then sanded until peaks are flat and pits remain. It reads as travertine or honed limestone, hides layer lines, and shows in flat office light, where shallow facets vanish (see wip-logo.png, nearly smooth head-on). It combines well with the facets: planes at 10 mm, pores at 0.3 mm.

## 4. Risks

- **Low-poly:** even size and even angle read as a mesh. Irregularity is the whole difference.
- **Contour rings:** near-horizontal crown facets show 0.2–0.5 mm layer steps as topographic rings; they must be sanded out.
- **Lighting:** matte white only shows facets at grazing light, so the temptation is to over-deepen until the form breaks.
- **Seam:** a crisp facet edge meeting the smooth front looks like two parts glued together.
- **Brow:** the brow and chin are already crisp; facets must not compete beside them.
