# Surface Critique — "The Pebble"

Graduated blue-liquid tubes read as lab glassware. In a pure-white smooth shell, that reads "hospital instrument." You need mineral tactility—without slipping into 3D-print kitsch.

## 1. Ranking

1. **(5) WIP reproduced** — Keeps the calm pebble silhouette and clean front bezel while breaking the clinical sheen with deliberate craft.
2. **(2) WIP (tessellation)** — Proved the instinct in PLA, but looks accidental: mesh triangulation, whittled-wood striping, and facets bleeding into the slots.
3. **(1) Smooth (A)** — Purest form and zero kitsch risk, yet clinical in white PLA; harsh sanding alone just scuffs a CAD blob.
4. **(4) River (undulation)** — Reads as warped, cellulite-like molding defects; real river stones have macro-asymmetry, not 15 mm lumps.
5. **(3) Facets (~8 mm, 1 mm ridges)** — Shatters the soft silhouette into a dated "low-poly planter" with a clumsy step at the smooth front.

## 2. Pick & Tuning

Pick **(5) WIP reproduced**, scaled slightly up as a sanding allowance—matte PLA sands fast, and ~2.6 mm planes will vanish under harsh grit.

- **Geometry:** **4–5 mm** varied facets (3.5–6 mm), **0.25–0.35 mm** outward ridges (~10–14° breaks). Deep enough to survive sanding as soft, worn stone; shallow enough to avoid unsanded valleys.
- **Fades:** **100%** on flanks/back. Feather over **5–6 mm** to **0%** around the front bezel/chin (a carved optical window). Taper to **30–40%** on the top dome (where 0.08 mm stair-stepping needs heavier sanding) and **0%** on the bottom **6 mm** for a crisp stance.

## 3. Better Idea

**Honed-limestone micro-grain + macro-asymmetry:** Break left-right symmetry by 1–2 mm (pebbles aren't CAD-mirrored) and add outward mineral grain (**0.15 mm** high, **0.8–1.2 mm** pitch, smooth front bezel). Harsh sanding shears peaks into silky plateaus beside matte micro-valleys—authentic honed travertine that erases 0.08 mm layer lines without reading as wood or low-poly.

## 4. Risks

- **Low-poly kitsch:** Facets breaking the silhouette (>5.5 mm or >0.4 mm ridge) fight the refined tubes.
- **Sanding trap:** Too shallow vanishes; too deep leaves burnished crests over dusty valleys.
- **Wood-grain clash:** Vertical facets amplify 0.08 mm layer lines (and won't hide the slot-top bridge seam).
- **Clinical white:** Cool white PLA still whispers "medical"—warm bone/limestone white does half the work.
