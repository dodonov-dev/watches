FLUSH CURVED FRONT, NO ALCOVE. The roly-poly's outline stays, but there is no recessed alcove at all: the front is the body's own smooth, gently curved surface (flattened just enough where the tubes are), and the two slot channels are cut directly into it, like slits in a smooth pebble. Calm, minimal, the tubes are the only feature. The fine joint line low on the body stays.
