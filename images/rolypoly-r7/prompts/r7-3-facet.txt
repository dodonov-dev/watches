SLICED FACET. The roly-poly's outline stays, but the face is a single flat oval facet, as if the front of the soft body had been sliced off with one clean cut: a smooth flat oval plane, about 50 mm wide and 92 mm tall, meeting the curved body in a crisp but softly broken edge, slightly set back at its centre. The two slot channels are cut into this flat facet. The fine joint line low on the body stays.
